# Benchmark results: Python loops vs NumCompute

Generated by `python -m benchmark.run_benchmarks` (seed 42, 7 repeats per version, 6.2 s in total). Times are the median per call. Baseline/result gives the Python-loop time and, in parentheses, the speed-up (loop time / NumCompute time). Both versions are checked to give the same result before they are timed.

## Environment

- python: 3.14.7
- numpy: 2.5.4
- os: Windows-11-10.0.26200-SP0
- cpu: Intel64 Family 6 Model 154 Stepping 4, GenuineIntel
- cpu_count: 12
